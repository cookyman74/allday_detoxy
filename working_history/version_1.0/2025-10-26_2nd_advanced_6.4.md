#### 1.4 Lint 검증

**실행 명령어**:
```bash
$ ./gradlew lint
```

**초기 결과**: ❌ **BUILD FAILED**

**에러 내용**:
```
/Users/junghojang/Developments/myProject/allday_detoxy/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml:27: 
Error: Remove androidx.work.WorkManagerInitializer from your AndroidManifest.xml 
when using on-demand initialization. [RemoveWorkManagerInitializer from androidx.work]
    <application
    ^
```

**원인**:
- `DetoxyApplication`이 `Configuration.Provider`를 구현
- WorkManager의 on-demand initialization 사용
- 기본 WorkManagerInitializer를 명시적으로 제거해야 함

**수정 사항**:
`app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml`에 다음 추가:
```xml
<!-- Disable WorkManager auto-initialization (using Configuration.Provider) -->
<provider
    android:name="androidx.startup.InitializationProvider"
    android:authorities="${applicationId}.androidx-startup"
    android:exported="false"
    tools:node="merge">
    <meta-data
        android:name="androidx.work.WorkManagerInitializer"
        android:value="androidx.startup"
        tools:node="remove" />
</provider>
```

**수정 후 결과**: ✅ **BUILD SUCCESSFUL in 15s**

**최종 상태**:
- **에러**: 0개
- **경고**: 131개 (Divider deprecated 등 기존 경고)
